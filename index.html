Questions

What is Chuply?

Chuply is a South African software company. It builds simple, Rand-priced tools for South African businesses and schools: ShopFront SA (online stores), Chuply Ops (field service management), Chuply Schools (cashless tuckshops) and Chuply Echo (personalised songs and voiceovers).

What is a good online store builder for South Africa that takes PayFast?

ShopFront SA by Chuply is an online store builder made for South African businesses. PayFast, Instant EFT and local couriers are built in, there is no extra transaction fee stacked on top, and pricing is in Rands from R0/month.

Is there job card and invoicing software for South African contractors?

Chuply Ops is field service management software for South African contractors and trades. It handles quotes, work cards, invoices and payments in one pipeline and is ZAR and VAT ready.

How can a South African school run a cashless tuckshop?

Chuply Schools lets students pay at the tuckshop with a QR card while parents top up online via PayFast. School admins see every transaction. It is free for up to 30 students.

Where can I get a personalised song with someone's name in it?

Chuply Echo makes a one-of-a-kind song with a person's name in it in minutes, shared with a link. It also makes natural voiceovers and radio ads for businesses, with South African English and Afrikaans voices.

Are Chuply products billed in Rands?

Yes. Every Chuply product is priced in South African Rands (ZAR), with no USD billing or conversion surprises. Products connect to PayFast, Ozow, Yoco, SnapScan and EFT.

Do I need to install an app to use Chuply?

No. Every Chuply product works in the web browser, shares over WhatsApp, and sets up in minutes with no downloads.
